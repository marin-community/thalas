# The Thalas Authors

The Thalas Authors currently include:

- The Board of Trustees of the Leland Stanford Junior University
- Open Athena

## History

The Thalas executor framework was originally developed as part of the [Marin] project by researchers at Stanford University's Center for Research on Foundation Models (CRFM).

This repository contains the executor framework extracted from Marin, with the original commit history preserved. The authors now maintain and develop this standalone version for broader use cases.

[Marin]: https://github.com/marin-community/marin
